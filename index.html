Motor Takaful Policy

The Comprehensive Cover of this policy covers participant against loss or damage to all types of vehicles (Tractors / Heavy Vehicles / Motor cars etc.) or its accessories which might occur as a result of accidental collision or overturning etc. or by fire, external explosion, self-ignition or lightening and theft or by malicious act.

Takaful Investment Plan

This type of Takaful is meant for anybody that needs to build capital. It is achieved by saving some amount of money in the plan for the purpose of investment under the Mudarabah principle (profit/loss Islamic investment principle) which will earn such participant accrued profit/loss in the future.

Get Quote

Marine Cargo Takaful Policy

This is a protection cover that provides cover on goods been transported internationally from one country into Nigeria either by sea or air. It covers loss as a result of accident, damage, fire or theft. This is a special plan designed to protect goods imported into the country (i.e. spare parts).
