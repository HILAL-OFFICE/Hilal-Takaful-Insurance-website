Prof. Tajudeen Olalekan Yusuf

Advisory Council Member

Professor of Insurance & Islamic Finance, UNILAG

Biography & Background

Prof. Tajudeen Olalekan Yusuf is an internationally acclaimed academician and scholar in the fields of Islamic Finance, Risk Management, and Takaful. He serves in the Department of Actuarial Science and Insurance, Faculty of Management Sciences, University of Lagos.

He holds a Ph.D. in Insurance and Risk Management from Nottingham University, United Kingdom, and an M.Sc. and B.Sc. in Insurance from the University of Lagos. Prof. Yusuf has authored numerous research papers and seminal textbooks on ethical Shariah insurance mechanisms and participatory risk pooling.

Imam Isma'eel Busaeri

Advisory Council Member

Islamic Law Jurisprudence & Shariah Auditor

Biography & Background

Imam Isma'eel Busaeri is a revered Islamic scholar and jurist with deep expertise in Islamic Commercial Jurisprudence (Fiqh al-Mu'amalat) and charitable wealth redistribution (Zakat and Waqf).

He holds an LL.B in Islamic Law, a B.A. in English Language & Literature, and an M.A. in Islamic Studies with specialized research in Islamic Finance and socio-economic poverty eradication models.

Barr. Muhammed Nurudeen Lawal

Advisory Council Member

Legal Practitioner, Shariah Advocate & Solicitor

Biography & Background

Barr. Muhammed Nurudeen Lawal is an accomplished legal practitioner and Shariah advisory consultant. He holds both an LL.B and LL.M in Law from Obafemi Awolowo University (OAU), Ile-Ife, and was called to the Nigerian Bar (B.L) after graduating from the Nigerian Law School, Abuja.

His legal practice focuses on non-interest commercial litigation, Shariah compliance contracts, corporate arbitration, and regulatory frameworks governing Takaful institutions.
