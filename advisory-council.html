Prof. Tajudeen Olalekan Yusuf

Advisory Council Member

Professor, Actuarial Science & Insurance, UNILAG

Biography & Background

Prof. Tajudeen Olalekan Yusuf is one of Africa's foremost authorities on Islamic Insurance (Takaful), Actuarial Risk Sciences, and Non-Interest Financial Engineering. He is a senior Professor in the Department of Actuarial Science and Insurance at the University of Lagos (UNILAG).

Prof. Yusuf obtained his Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) from the prestigious University of Nottingham, United Kingdom, and holds both his Master of Science (M.Sc) and Bachelor of Science (B.Sc) in Management and Insurance from the University of Lagos.

He has authored extensive pioneering research papers on Takaful underwriting models, Waqf foundations, and Mudarabah/Wakala fund governance. On the Advisory Council of Hilal Takaful, he provides authoritative guidance on Shariah contract alignment and surplus distribution formulas.

Imam Isma'eel Busaeri

Advisory Council Member

LLB (Islamic Law), BA (English), MA (Islamic Studies)

Biography & Background

Imam Isma'eel Busaeri is a respected Islamic scholar, jurist, and consultant specializing in Fiqh al-Mu'amalat (Islamic Commercial Jurisprudence), ethical finance, and wealth preservation.

He holds an LLB in Islamic Law (Shariah), a Bachelor of Arts in English Language and Literature, and a Master of Arts in Islamic Studies focused particularly on Non-Interest Finance, Zakat, Waqf, and economic empowerment.

He serves as a pivotal Shariah resource person and consultant to financial institutions, ensuring that insurance operations are free from Riba (usury/interest), Gharar (excessive ambiguity), and Maysir (gambling).

Barr. Muhammad I. Lawal

Advisory Council Member

Legal Consultant in English & Islamic Law (Since 1992)

Biography & Background

Barrister Muhammad I. Lawal is an eminent Nigerian legal practitioner and Shariah advocate with over three decades of distinguished court practice and legal consultancy dating back to 1992.

With deep mastery of both Nigerian Statutory Common Law and classical Islamic Shariah Jurisprudence, Barr. Lawal represents a bridge between contemporary statutory mandates (such as NAICOM regulations) and sacred Islamic contractual stipulations.

At Hilal Takaful, Barr. Lawal ensures that every commercial contract, reinsurance treaty, and participant agreement satisfies both national insurance legislation and ethical Shariah legal tenets.
