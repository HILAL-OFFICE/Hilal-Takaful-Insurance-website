Lanre Hassan

Managing Director / CEO

FIIN / ACIIN, ACIMN, AMNIM

Biography & Background

Lanre Hassan serves as the Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer of Hilal Takaful Nigeria Limited. He is a Fellow of the Chartered Insurance Institute of Nigeria (ACIIN), an Associate of the Chartered Institute of Marketing of Nigeria (ACIMN), and an Associate Member of the Nigerian Institute of Management (AMNIM).

With an exceptional career exceeding 20 years, Mr. Hassan has established a stellar reputation for visionary leadership, technological innovation in policy distribution, and robust participant fund administration.

He leads the daily executive operations of Hilal Takaful, instilling the core values of Professionalism, Innovation, Integrity, Empathy, and Team Spirit across all branch networks and digital channels.

Tajudeen Olusesi

Head, Business Development

M.Sc Risk Mgt & Insurance, B.Sc Accounting, CIBN

Biography & Background

Tajudeen Olabanji Olusesi heads the Business Development division at Hilal Takaful Nigeria Limited. He holds a Bachelor of Science (B.Sc) in Accounting and a Master of Science (M.Sc) in Risk Management and Insurance, both from the University of Lagos.

Mr. Olusesi is a credentialed member of the Chartered Institute of Bankers of Nigeria (CIBN). His dual background in accounting and risk analytics enables him to structure tailored corporate insurance solutions that align with client risk profiles.

He spearheads corporate and commercial client acquisition, broker relationships, bancatakaful channels, and public sector partnerships across Nigeria.

Ibrahim Jimoh

Team Lead, Family Underwriting

Ex-Cornerstone Insurance PLC (Since 2009)

Biography & Background

Ibrahim Jimoh leads the Family Takaful Underwriting Unit at Hilal Takaful Nigeria Limited. He brings over 16 years of specialized insurance underwriting experience, having commenced his career at Cornerstone Insurance PLC in 2009.

During his long tenure in non-interest life and family risk pooling, Mr. Jimoh has designed and evaluated numerous group life, mortgage protection, education savings, and pilgrimage care schemes.

He ensures that mortality pricing, underwriting underwriting surplus calculations, and participant savings accounts are managed with mathematical precision and Shariah adherence.

Adekoya Shittu Olusegun

Team Lead, General Underwriting

Pioneer Cornerstone Takaful Specialist

Biography & Background

Shittu Olusegun Adekoya serves as Team Lead for the General Business Underwriting Unit. He commands over 12 years of core industry experience with dedicated specialization in Islamic insurance since the inception of Cornerstone Takaful.

Mr. Adekoya possesses vast technical expertise in non-life underwriting, risk surveying, rate negotiation, and loss control for Motor, Engineering All Risks, Fire & Special Perils, Marine Cargo, and Public Liability.

He is instrumental in deploying Hilal's instant motor certificate generation platform and ensuring that corporate assets are protected under ethical Tabarru agreements.

Hafsat Shuaib Umar

Proposed Head, Legal & Compliance

Legal & Compliance Officer, Hilal Takaful

Biography & Background

Hafsat Shuaib Umar is the Legal and Compliance Officer (Proposed Head of Legal & Compliance) at Hilal Takaful Nigeria Limited. She holds a Bachelor of Laws (LLB) degree and is a Barrister and Solicitor of the Supreme Court of Nigeria.

Ms. Umar provides high-level legal counsel on regulatory matters, corporate governance guidelines, statutory filings with NAICOM, and contractual due diligence across all institutional engagements.

She also coordinates the Anti-Money Laundering and Countering Financing of Terrorism (AML/CFT) framework, ensuring Hilal maintains impeccable regulatory standing.

Sulaimon Adesegun Ibidunni

Proposed Head of Finance

Financial Professional & Audit Specialist

Biography & Background

Sulaimon Adesegun Ibidunni is the financial professional serving as Proposed Head of Finance at Hilal Takaful Nigeria Limited. He brings over eight years of comprehensive experience in Internal Audit, Financial Accounting, and Statutory Reporting.

His deep understanding of financial analytics and audit control safeguards the rigorous separation between Shareholders’ Funds and Participants’ Takaful Funds (PTF) required by Shariah standards and NAICOM regulations.

Mr. Ibidunni manages general ledger accounting, treasury management, tax administration, and the annual actuarial surplus disbursement process.
