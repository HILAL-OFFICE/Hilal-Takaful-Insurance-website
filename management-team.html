Abdur-Rasheed Abiodun Babalola, mni

Managing Director, Hilal Takaful Insurance Company Limited

Fiduciary Leader & Financial Inclusion Executive

Biography & Background

Abdur-Rasheed Abiodun Babalola, mni, is a financial services executive with over 20 years of experience in Takaful, Islamic finance, general insurance, microinsurance, and financial inclusion. As Managing Director of Hilal Takaful Insurance Company Limited, he is focused on expanding access to ethical, Shariah-compliant insurance solutions and promoting sustainable economic growth.

He also co-developed innovative microinsurance products approved by the National Insurance Commission (NAICOM), helping to extend insurance coverage to underserved communities.

Beyond his corporate leadership, he is passionate about mentorship and transformational leadership, having mentored and advised over 1,000 emerging leaders. He also served as a Non-Executive Director of Arthur Group, a non-interest financial asset management company.

He holds a B.Sc. in Insurance and a Master’s degree in Managerial Psychology from the University of Lagos and is an Associate Member of the Chartered Insurance Institute of Nigeria. His leadership reflects a commitment to strengthening Takaful, advancing financial inclusion, and delivering sustainable value to individuals, businesses, and communities.

Folashade Alice Oyekan (FCA)

Head of Finance

Fellow Chartered Accountant (FCA) & Financial Governance Executive

Biography & Background

Folashade Alice Oyekan is a Chartered Accountant (FCA) and finance executive with 22 years of progressive experience in Nigeria’s insurance industry, spanning both conventional and takaful insurance. She leads financial planning, reporting, capital management, and investment oversight as Head of Finance at Hilal Takaful Insurance Company Limited.

Her career began in treasury and management accounting at Royal Exchange General Insurance, where she spent 18 years and rose to Head of Reporting & Tax. There she strengthened financial reporting structures for NAICOM approval, built a credit-control unit, and reduced bad debt by about ₦100 million through coordinated collection efforts. She later served as Head of Accounts at Staco Insurance Plc before advancing into executive CFO leadership in January 2024.

She is known for producing timely, accurate financial statements under IFRS and for maintaining strong standing with NAICOM and other regulators. Her budgeting, forecasting, and risk analysis guide profitable growth, while her management of capital structure, liquidity, and investments grows returns on premium income and keeps funds available for claims. She brings strong internal controls, audit readiness, and rigorous account reconciliation to every role, and she works with boards, investors, auditors, and regulators to build trust and secure favourable terms.

Folashade holds an MBA in Financial Management from Lagos State University and a Higher National Diploma in Accounting. Her professional memberships include Fellow of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria (ICAN), the Institute of Strategic Management of Nigeria, and the Chartered Institute of Loan & Risk Management of Nigeria. She has also published research on accounting ratios as a measure of management efficiency.
