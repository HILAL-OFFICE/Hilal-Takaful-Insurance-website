Our Whistleblowing Commitment

At Hilal Takaful Nigeria Limited, upholding the highest standards of transparency, integrity, and Shariah compliance is our sacred responsibility (Amanah). We provide a safe, protected reporting mechanism for staff, participants, vendors, and the general public to report any concern without fear of victimization or retaliation.

Confidentiality Guarantee: All disclosures submitted through this portal are treated with absolute confidentiality. Reports are received directly by the Head of Internal Audit and the Board Audit & Governance Committee.
