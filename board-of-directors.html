Bashir Haliru Binji

Chairman, Board of Directors

CEO, FIN Insurance Company Limited

Biography & Background

Mr. Bashir Haliru Binji is a distinguished corporate leader, insurance maven, and strategic visionary who presides as Chairman of the Board of Directors at Hilal Takaful Nigeria Limited. With more than twenty-five years of accomplished leadership within Nigeria's insurance sector, Mr. Binji brings immense statutory authority and strategic acumen.

He currently serves as the Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer of FIN Insurance Company Limited, where he has successfully directed broad institutional restructuring, strengthened capital adequacy, and introduced technology-driven operating models.

At Hilal Takaful, Mr. Binji steers board governance, statutory compliance with NAICOM regulations, and guides strategic partnerships that propel the company's status as Nigeria's premier composite Takaful window and standalone operator.

Peter Ekwueme

Non-Executive Director

Senior Insurance Executive & Strategist

Biography & Background

Peter Ekwueme is a veteran insurance executive with an illustrious career spanning more than 28 years in general business underwriting, reinsurance treaty placement, public-sector risks, and corporate market expansion.

Throughout his extensive career across top-tier insurance underwriting companies in Nigeria, Mr. Ekwueme has directed major commercial portfolios and spearheaded corporate restructuring initiatives that created enduring shareholder and policyholder value.

As a Non-Executive Director at Hilal Takaful, he provides rigorous oversight on underwriting risk quality, investment policies, customer retention frameworks, and corporate expansion strategies.

Olalekan Moshood

Non-Executive Director

Head, Technical Division, FIN Insurance Co. Ltd

Biography & Background

Olalekan Moshood possesses over 22 years of rich, technical domain expertise in core insurance operations, specializing in Underwriting, Claims Administration, Reinsurance Treaties, Risk Management, and Insurance Broking.

Prior to and during his leadership as Head of the Technical Division at FIN Insurance Company Limited, he formulated underwriting rating guidelines and managed complex risk treaties across marine, aviation, engineering, and oil & gas lines.

His deep understanding of claims turnaround efficiency and actuarial risk pool stability plays a vital role in Hilal Takaful's ability to maintain prompt, dispute-free claims payouts.

Abubakar Sadik Adam

Independent Non-Executive Director

Ex-Marketing Officer II, Nigeria Airways Ltd

Biography & Background

Abubakar Sadik Adam is a revered corporate statesman whose career spans four decades of high-level service in both public enterprises and private corporate leadership.

Having commenced his executive journey at Nigeria Airways Ltd as Marketing Officer II, Mr. Adam accumulated extensive experience in commercial operations, regulatory compliance, public logistics, and corporate administration.

As an Independent Non-Executive Director, Mr. Adam serves as an impartial guardian of transparency and participant fairness. He focuses on protecting the Participants' Takaful Fund (PTF) and ensuring equitable, non-conflicted governance.

Lanre Hassan

Managing Director / CEO

FIIN / ACIIN, ACIMN, AMNIM

Biography & Background

Lanre Hassan is an energetic insurance titan and leading exponent of Islamic non-interest finance in West Africa. He is a Fellow of the Chartered Insurance Institute of Nigeria (FIIN / ACIIN), an Associate of the Chartered Institute of Marketing of Nigeria (ACIMN), and an Associate Member of the Nigerian Institute of Management (AMNIM).

With more than twenty years of strategic leadership across general underwriting, life insurance, and corporate risk broking, Mr. Hassan has been the driving engine behind Hilal Takaful's transformation into Nigeria's most trusted composite Takaful brand.

Under his executive direction, Hilal Takaful achieved notable milestones including seven consecutive years of audited surplus distribution, digital instant claims settlements, and nationwide financial inclusion outreach.
