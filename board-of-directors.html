Abdur-Rasheed Babalola

Managing Director

Fiduciary Leader & Financial Inclusion Executive

Biography & Background

Abdur-Rasheed Abiodun Babalola, mni, is a financial services executive with over 20 years of experience in Takaful, Islamic finance, general insurance, microinsurance, and financial inclusion. As Managing Director of Hilal Takaful Insurance Company Limited, he is focused on expanding access to ethical, Shariah-compliant insurance solutions and promoting sustainable economic growth.

He also co-developed innovative microinsurance products approved by the National Insurance Commission (NAICOM), helping to extend insurance coverage to underserved communities.

Beyond his corporate leadership, he is passionate about mentorship and transformational leadership, having mentored and advised over 1,000 emerging leaders. He also served as a Non-Executive Director of Arthur Group, a non-interest financial asset management company.

He holds a B.Sc. in Insurance and a Master’s degree in Managerial Psychology from the University of Lagos and is an Associate Member of the Chartered Insurance Institute of Nigeria. His leadership reflects a commitment to strengthening Takaful, advancing financial inclusion, and delivering sustainable value to individuals, businesses, and communities.

Peter Ekwueme

Non-Executive Director

Biography & Background

Peter Ekwueme is a highly accomplished insurance professional currently serving as the Executive Director of Technical Operations at Cornerstone Insurance Plc. With an impressive career spanning over 28 years, he has consistently demonstrated his commitment to excellence and expertise in various aspects of the insurance industry.

Through his dedication and exceptional leadership, he climbed the corporate ladder, holding roles of increasing responsibility. His tenure included a crucial role as the Executive Director of Technical Operations at FIN Insurance Company Limited before returning to Cornerstone. Notably, as the General Manager and Divisional Head, he oversaw the Institutional Business unit, managing sales and operations for the Special Risk segment across multiple regions.

His extensive experience, leadership prowess, and dedication to excellence make him an invaluable asset to the insurance industry. His journey from a Graduate Trainee in 1995 to an Executive Director exemplifies his remarkable career trajectory and commitment to the field. In his current role at Cornerstone Insurance Plc, he continues to drive technical operations, ensuring the highest standards of service and performance. Furthermore, his contributions and impact on the insurance sector underscore his status as a visionary and accomplished leader, shaping the future of insurance in Nigeria.

He possesses a solid educational foundation, with a Bachelor's degree in Agriculture from the University of Agriculture Makurdi and a Master's in Business Administration from Delta State University Abraka. His commitment to professional growth is evident in his affiliation as an Associate member of the Chartered Insurance Institute of Nigeria (CIIN). Furthermore, Peter's dedication to continuous learning is underscored by his participation in various courses, including an Advanced Management Course on Sales Force Performance Management at the renowned Kellogg School of Management in Illinois, Chicago, USA. His association with the Kellogg School of Management and the Lagos Business School reflects his proactive approach to staying abreast of industry developments.

Serah Makka

Independent Non-Executive Director

Biography & Background

Serah Makka is an accomplished executive and governance professional with over 20 years of international experience spanning public policy, development finance, financial inclusion, institutional reform and strategic leadership.

She currently serves as Executive Director for Africa at the ONE Campaign, where she leads the organisation's regional strategy and advocacy efforts to mobilise investment, strengthen economic and financial systems, and advance inclusive growth across the continent. Her work focuses on addressing structural barriers to development, including the cost of capital, domestic resource mobilisation and access to financing for underserved communities.

Throughout her career, Serah has worked at the intersection of policy, finance and institutional development, engaging governments, multilateral institutions, the private sector and civil society to advance reforms and strengthen accountability. Her experience includes working with Enhancing Financial Innovation & Access (EFInA), an organisation dedicated to expanding access to financial services for underserved populations in Nigeria.

Serah was recognised in 2012 as one of the Global Top 35 Under 35 in International Affairs and is an Archbishop Desmond Tutu Leadership Fellow.

She also teaches governance and civic participation at the Fix Politics School of Public Policy and Governance and serves on the boards of Future Perspectives, Resilience Action Network Africa (RANA) and the Institute for Public Policy Research in the United Kingdom.

Serah holds a Bachelor's degree in Economics from the University of North Florida and a Master's degree in Public Policy from Harvard Kennedy School.

Comrade Quadri Olaleye

Independent Non-Executive Director

FNIM,FCIA

Biography & Background

Comrade Quadri Olaleye is a business and management professional with experience and interests spanning organisational leadership, business development, strategic management, risk and resilience, and institutional development. He brings a multidisciplinary perspective to governance, combining professional training, strategic insight, stakeholder engagement and a strong commitment to sustainable business growth.

His professional development reflects a keen interest in emerging business trends, organisational performance and strategic resilience. His training includes Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and Optimisation, Business Innovation through Artificial Intelligence (AI) and the Internet of Things (IoT), Disaster Management, and National Security and Country Resilience. These areas of specialisation provide a strong foundation for contributing to organisational efficiency, business transformation, risk management and long-term institutional sustainability.

Quadri is a Fellow of the Nigerian Institute of Management (Chartered) (FNIM) and the Chartered Institute of Administration (FCIA). He is also a certified member of the Real Estate Developers Association of Nigeria (REDAN) and maintains professional affiliations with the Lagos Chamber of Commerce and Industry and the Abuja Chamber of Commerce and Industry. He is an alumnus of the Galilee International Management Institute and its Institute of Security and Strategic Studies.

Beyond his professional pursuits, Quadri is committed to social impact, particularly in the areas of education, youth empowerment and community development. Through his leadership engagements, he has supported initiatives that provide financial assistance to high-performing graduates and create opportunities for talented and vulnerable young people to pursue their ambitions.

As a prospective Independent Non-Executive Director, Quadri seeks to contribute to effective corporate governance, strategic direction and robust board oversight. His areas of board-level interest include strategy and business development, finance and investment, risk management and resilience, stakeholder relations, human capital development, business transformation, strategic partnerships and corporate social responsibility.

Through his professional affiliations, commitment to continuous learning and dedication to community development, Quadri seeks to contribute meaningfully to institutions that value accountability, sustainable growth, sound governance and long-term stakeholder value.
